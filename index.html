03 What I do

Reliable and secure are the same job.

Most teams treat uptime, security, and compliance as three separate problems. I build them into the same Terraform, the same pipelines, and the same alerts.

Reliability engineering

SLOs that mean something, alerting that fires before customers notice, and recovery that's rehearsed instead of improvised.

  • SLOs/SLIs for 20+ critical services
  • 40% faster incident detection
  • DR playbooks with a 2-hour RTO

Shift-left security

Security scanning and secrets management built into CI/CD, so problems are caught at the pull request, not in production.

  • 40+ security controls in IaC pipelines
  • Hardcoded credentials eliminated
  • Snyk, Wiz, and HashiCorp Vault

Compliance as code

Policy-as-code Terraform modules for PCI DSS environments, so audit evidence is a by-product of shipping, not a yearly scramble.

  • 100% compliance, 3 audit cycles running
  • 75% faster provisioning
  • 500+ endpoints under continuous monitoring

09 Answers

Questions hiring teams ask me.

The honest answers, before you have to ask.

Thirteen years at one company. Can you adapt somewhere new?

It's been five different jobs, not one. Each role brought new tools, new teams and new stakeholders: support, accounts, data, DevOps, then DevSecOps. The infrastructure itself spans AWS, Azure and GCP, so I'm used to working across platforms rather than inside one.

Are the numbers on this page real?

Yes. They come from production systems at Global Payments, and they match my résumé. Pick any one and I'll walk you through how it was measured and what changed to move it.

Are you hands-on, or do you mostly manage?

Hands-on. I write the Terraform modules, build the pipelines and work the incidents. The portfolio repository has 13 projects you can read today. I've also mentored four junior engineers, so I can lead without leaving the keyboard.

Can you work in a regulated environment?

It's the environment I work in now: PCI DSS payment infrastructure, with compliance held across three straight audit cycles. Compliance-as-code is one of the public projects.

Your degree is in mass communication. Is the technical foundation there?

I followed the B.A. from LSU with an M.S. in Information Technology Management from UT Dallas, Microsoft and Google Cloud certifications, and years of production work on top. The communication background shows up in clear runbooks and incident write-ups.
